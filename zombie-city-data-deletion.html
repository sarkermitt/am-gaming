Zombie City account & data deletion

AM-Games · Last updated: October 8, 2026

These instructions apply to Zombie City (com.amgames.zombiecity) by AM-Games. You can request deletion of your game account and associated cloud save without reinstalling the game or signing in to this website.

Request deletion by email

Email amgames.developer@gmail.com, preferably from the Google account address you used in Zombie City.

Subject: Zombie City account and data deletion request

Email deletion request

This opens your email app. Review and send the message to submit your request. If the button does not open an email app, copy the address and subject above into your email service.

What to include

  • The game name, Zombie City, and the Google account email used to sign in.
  • Whether you want the entire game account and cloud save deleted or a particular record, such as a support message.
  • For an advertising or diagnostic-data request, include the approximate dates and device model if known, so we can assess what records can be identified.

If you no longer have access to the account email, explain this so we can discuss ownership verification. Do not send passwords, verification codes or authentication tokens.

We will reply with any verification steps and the expected completion time, and confirm when the request is handled. If records must be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable period.

What account deletion covers

  • Your Zombie City Firebase Authentication account and the associated sign-in profile.
  • Your associated cloud save: money, gems, level stars, selected level, gun upgrades and skin, daily-reward claim status and save metadata.

Deleting the cloud account removes access to that backup. It does not delete your Google account.

What is handled separately

  • Progress on your devices: local copies remain until you clear storage on each device.
  • Advertising and technical records: these belong to separate service systems and are not automatically removed with the game account. Mention them in your request. We will explain the available provider deletion options and limits.
  • Support messages: include these in your request if you also want your email correspondence or attachments removed.
  • Provider backups or required records: may remain for the provider's retention period or a specific legal or security requirement. See the privacy policy's retention information.

Signing out is not account deletion

From Home, open User beside Gun Upgrade, or tap your profile picture if signed in, then choose Sign Out. This stops cloud backup on that device while retaining your local progress and cloud account. The current game does not have an in-app account-deletion button; use the email request above.

Sign out on your devices while a deletion request is being processed. Signing in again after deletion can create a new game account and back up progress still present on a device.

Clear progress from your device

  1. If you want both cloud and local information removed, request cloud-account deletion first and wait for confirmation.
  2. On Android, open Settings > Apps > Zombie City > Storage or Storage & cache.
  3. Choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device.

This resets local money, gems, level results, gun upgrades, daily-reward state and preferences. Repeat on other devices that hold a copy. Android backup and restore settings may restore local data later. Clearing storage or uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data.
