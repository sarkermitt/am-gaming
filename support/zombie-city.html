Gameplay help

Why is the next level locked?

Win the previous level to unlock it. Cedar Crossing contains levels 1–10; Nightfall District contains levels 11–20. A win requires clearing the zombies while keeping at least one human alive.

How do I stop running?

The Run button toggles running. Tap once to activate it, then tap again to stop.

Can I change the touch controls?

Open Settings > Controls > Customize to move and resize them. You can also adjust sensitivity, choose left-handed controls under Display, and review the shooting-assist options.

How do gems and revives work?

Buy gem packs in the Shop using earned game money. A gem revive costs 5 gems. The Hero Down screen also offers Watch Ad when a rewarded ad is available.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

  1. Open User beside Gun Upgrade on Home, then choose Sign in with Google. When connected, your profile photo can replace the User icon.
  2. Make sure you have an internet connection and choose the same Google account used for your previous backup.
  3. Use Back Up Now to save device progress or Cloud Save to check the backup.
  4. If asked to choose between Use Device and Use Cloud, compare the displayed money, gems and stars carefully. Choosing a save replaces the other; it does not combine balances.

If sign-in or backup fails, keep your local data and email us the exact error. Avoid clearing storage or uninstalling while trying to recover a save that has not been backed up. Control and audio preferences currently stay on each device.

Rewarded ads and daily rewards

Ads need internet and may be unavailable. You receive the reward only after the ad service confirms completion; closing an ad early does not guarantee a reward. If an ad cannot load, try later or use the normal claim/retry options.

The daily reward is 200 game money, with an optional rewarded-ad choice for three times the amount. If a confirmed reward appears missing, include the approximate date and time, whether it was a daily reward or revive, and a description of what happened.

Where required, revisit your ad choices using Settings > Ad Privacy.

Loading or performance problems

Close other demanding apps, restart the game and check that the device has free storage. The current Android build targets Android 8.0 or later on 64-bit ARM devices. For a repeatable crash or loading problem, send the steps and any error shown. Do not clear local data unless you intend to reset it or have confirmed a usable cloud backup.

Privacy and account removal

Sign Out disconnects cloud backup; it does not delete your game account. Visit the account and data-deletion page to request removal or read the privacy policy for data-handling details.
