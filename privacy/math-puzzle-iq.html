Privacy Policy

For Math Puzzle IQ · Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AM-Games ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Math Puzzle IQ mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional). If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID through Firebase Authentication. Signing in is optional and only used to save and sync your game progress.
  • Game progress. Your levels, stars, coins, IQ score and settings are stored on your device and, if you sign in, backed up to Cloud Firestore so you can restore them.
  • Usage & device data. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used (for example, levels started or completed, purchases and rewarded-ad views), along with standard device and app information such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers. Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID and related information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases (coin packs and the "Remove Ads" subscription) are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation of a purchase but never your full payment details. A random device identifier is used to keep the "Remove Ads" subscription active on a single device at a time.

How we use information

  • To provide and save your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To operate in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the App free.
  • To measure performance, fix bugs, and improve the game.

Third-party services

The App relies on the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play services & Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — AdMob & privacy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

The App shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Subscribing to "Remove Ads" turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention & your choices

  • You can play without signing in; in that case your progress stays only on your device.
  • Signing out removes your saved profile from the device. Uninstalling the App removes local data.
  • To request deletion of the game data linked to your account, email us at the address below and we will delete it from our cloud storage.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored using Google's secure infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
