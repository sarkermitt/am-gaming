Privacy Policy

For Jungle Hunt · Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how AM-Games handles information in connection with the Jungle Hunt mobile game. Contact us at sarkermit.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Game progress on your device

Jungle Hunt does not require an account or sign-in. The game stores your chapter progress, stars, coins, equipment upgrades, arrow inventory, selected arrow, best results and preferences locally on your device. These records let you continue where you left off.

Gameplay works without an internet connection. Optional Google sign-in lets you back up and restore progress across Android devices. There is no chat or user-upload feature.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google and Firebase Authentication process the sign-in and account identifiers. Firebase may receive the basic profile information associated with your Google sign-in, such as your name and email address. AM-Games uses the Firebase account identifier to store your game progress and preferences in Cloud Firestore. Cloud backup requires an internet connection.

The game does not receive your Google password. From Camp, use the user icon in the top-right corner beside Settings to open Google / Cloud Save. You can disconnect cloud backup or delete your Jungle Hunt cloud save and account there. This does not delete your Google account.

The cloud save includes chapter progress and best results, stars, coins, equipment upgrades, special-arrow counts, the equipped arrow, tutorial progress and preferences. If cloud and device saves conflict, the game asks which save to keep. It does not combine currency or ammunition from different saves.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Firebase Analytics processes gameplay events, such as chapter starts and results, tutorial completion and purchases made with game coins, along with app and device identifiers and technical information. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, exceptions, app and device information and diagnostic identifiers to help us investigate reliability problems. Our custom events do not include your name, email address or sign-in tokens. Android advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the game configuration.

Google provides these Firebase services. See Firebase privacy and security information and Google's Privacy Policy for the provider's data practices.

Technical diagnostics and Unity

Jungle Hunt is built with Unity. Unity runtime diagnostics are enabled in the current project configuration. Depending on the runtime and service configuration, Unity can receive technical diagnostic information such as crash logs, telemetry and configuration metadata to support performance, compatibility and reliability.

The types of data processed and their handling depend on Unity's enabled services and settings. This policy therefore does not represent Jungle Hunt as an app that never transmits technical data. See Unity's data collection information and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its practices and available privacy choices.

Advertising and purchases

Jungle Hunt uses Google AdMob for full-screen ads between level attempts, after a win or loss. An ad appears only when one is available; gameplay can continue if it cannot load. Viewing an ad does not award game items. Special arrows and equipment use coins earned through play; there are no real-money in-app purchases or requests for payment-card details.

Google's advertising SDK can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, app/device identifiers and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We request non-personalized ads and disable Android advertising-ID collection; this does not disable all advertising data processing. Where required, Google's consent message lets you make privacy choices, and Settings provides an Ad Privacy option to revisit them. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate reported problems and handle your request. Email is processed through our email service provider. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information or other information that is not needed for support.

Retention and deletion

Your local game progress remains on your device until it is cleared. Android backup and restore settings may affect whether that progress is restored. Clearing game storage does not remove support correspondence or diagnostic records that have already been transmitted.

Cloud progress remains associated with your Jungle Hunt account until you delete it. From Camp, tap the top-right user icon beside Settings, then choose Delete Cloud Account on the Google / Cloud Save screen. Tap Confirm Delete and verify with the same Google account. The game deletes your Firestore save and Firebase Authentication account; it keeps your local progress. Disconnecting, clearing app storage or uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data. Deleting your cloud account does not immediately erase previously transmitted analytics or crash records, whose handling follows the service settings and provider practices.

To request deletion of support information or ask about diagnostic records, contact sarkermit.apps@gmail.com. Identify the information involved so that we can locate it and respond. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing issue. Unity's handling and retention of information are described in its linked privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Play without creating an account.
  • Use optional Google sign-in for cloud backup, disconnect it, or delete your Jungle Hunt cloud account through the top-right user icon in Camp.
  • Where an Ad Privacy option is available in Settings, use it to revisit advertising privacy choices.
  • Change music, sound effects, aiming assistance and motion options in the game's Settings.
  • Use Android's app storage controls to clear local progress and preferences. See Jungle Hunt data-deletion instructions.
  • Contact us with a privacy request or use the privacy options described by Unity for information it handles.

Parents and guardians

Jungle Hunt contains stylized fantasy combat with arrows, bombs and fire effects. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information provided by a child, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

Updates and contact

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and revise the date above. For questions about this policy, email sarkermit.apps@gmail.com.
