Zombie City Privacy Policy

AM-Games · Last updated: October 8, 2026

AM-Games provides Zombie City, the Android game identified as com.amgames.zombiecity. This policy explains the information handled by the game, its connected services and our support team. Contact amgames.developer@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Google sign-in is optional. You can keep playing with a local save. Advertising and technical services may process information when you are online even if you have not signed in.

Request account or data deletion · Retention · Your choices

Progress on your device

Zombie City saves your money and gems, completed levels and best stars, selected level, gun upgrades and skin, daily-reward claim status, and control, sound, display and shooting preferences. Local storage allows you to continue playing without a cloud account. These settings and records stay on the device unless included in the cloud backup described below.

Optional Google sign-in

When you choose User > Sign in with Google from Home, Google and Firebase Authentication verify your account. The game receives your account identifier, email address and any display name and profile-photo URL supplied by Google. It uses these to identify your backup and show the connected account. Your profile photo is downloaded from its provider to display in the game. Zombie City does not receive your Google password.

Firebase handles the sign-in session. Choosing Sign Out disconnects your account on that device and keeps your local progress. It does not delete the account or cloud save.

Cloud backup

Cloud Firestore stores your game money, gems, level stars, selected level, gun upgrade levels, gun skin and daily-reward claim status, associated with your Firebase account identifier. Save-version, revision and update-time information help synchronize backups and detect conflicting saves.

We use this information to back up, restore and synchronize progress. The save is not a public profile or leaderboard. If device and cloud saves differ, the game asks which to keep; selecting one replaces the other. Control and audio preferences are not included in the current cloud backup.

Rewarded advertising

Zombie City uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. You can choose to watch an available ad to revive or multiply a daily reward. Ads are shown after you select the relevant Watch Ad option. The advertising service can initialize and request or preload ads before you choose to watch one, subject to its consent checks.

Google's advertising SDK processes IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; app and ad interactions; diagnostic information; and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

The game uses Google's consent interface when required. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, Settings > Ad Privacy lets you revisit those choices. Available options depend on your region and Google's requirements. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Choosing not to watch an ad does not itself prevent all SDK processing.

Shop packs and gun upgrades use in-game currency. The current game does not collect payment-card information or offer real-money purchases.

Technical information

Zombie City is built with Unity and has engine diagnostics enabled. Unity may process technical app, device, performance and error information to support engine reliability and compatibility. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its practices and available privacy choices.

The current game does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. This does not remove the advertising SDK's measurement or the technical processing described above.

Support and service providers

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer questions, investigate problems, verify account ownership and handle data requests. Send only what is needed; never send a password, verification code or sign-in token.

Google/Firebase provides sign-in and cloud storage; Google AdMob provides advertising; Google Gmail handles support email; and Unity provides engine technology and related diagnostics. These providers may process data in countries other than yours. Their linked policies describe their handling of information. See Firebase's privacy and security information.

When you visit this website, the hosting service receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, requested page and browser information to deliver and protect the site. The Zombie City pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising scripts or a website account system. Email links open your chosen email application.

Security

Cloud access uses your authenticated game account. Google and Firebase services use encrypted network connections. No online service or storage method can promise absolute security. Keep your Google account and device protected, and contact us if you believe someone has accessed your game account without permission.

Retention and deletion

  • Local progress: stays on your device until you clear app storage or otherwise remove it. Android backup and restore settings may restore a copy later.
  • Game account and cloud save: retained to provide your account and backup until deletion is completed. Request deletion of both your Zombie City Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore save by email.
  • Advertising and technical records: subject to the relevant provider's retention and deletion processes. Removing the game account does not automatically erase these separate records.
  • Support correspondence: kept as needed to handle your request and follow-up. You may request deletion of messages or attachments.

Provider backups and security or legally required records may have separate retention periods. We will explain any applicable limits when handling a request. Firebase's linked privacy information describes provider-specific retention. We do not promise that deleting an active account immediately removes every provider backup.

Your choices

You can play without signing in, sign out of cloud backup, revisit available ad privacy choices, clear local storage, or request access, correction, a copy or deletion of identifiable information. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact amgames.developer@gmail.com to make a request; we may need to verify account ownership.

The current User popup has Sign Out but no account-deletion button. Use the Zombie City account and data-deletion page to send a request without installing the game. Uninstalling or signing out alone does not remove your cloud account. Your Google account is separate and is not deleted by a Zombie City data request.

Parents and guardians

Zombie City includes stylized violence, guns and blood effects. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or a support message, contact us so we can investigate and address the request. Google sign-in is also subject to Google's account requirements.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when the game's data practices change. Privacy contact: AM-Games, amgames.developer@gmail.com.
