Delete your Jungle Hunt account and data

AM-Games · Last updated: September 14, 2026

This page is for Jungle Hunt (com.amgames.junglehunt) by AM-Games. You can delete your cloud account in the game or request deletion by email. You do not need to reinstall the game or sign in to this website to send a request.

Request deletion by email

Send your request to sarkermit.apps@gmail.com, preferably from the Google account email used for Jungle Hunt.

Subject: Jungle Hunt account and data deletion request

Email deletion request

This button opens your email app. Review and send the message to submit your request. If it does not open, copy the address and subject into your email service.

What to include

  • The game name, Jungle Hunt, and the Google email address used to sign in.
  • Whether you want to delete the whole cloud account and save, or particular data such as a support message.
  • For diagnostic-data requests, the approximate dates, game version and device model if known. We will explain what records we can locate and what provider deletion options are available.

If you cannot access the account email, explain that in your message so we can discuss ownership verification. Never send a password, one-time code or authentication token.

We will reply with any ownership-verification steps and the expected completion time, and confirm when the request has been handled. If certain records cannot be removed, we will explain why and how long they must be kept.

Delete the cloud account in the game

  1. Connect to the internet and return to Camp.
  2. Tap the user icon at the top right, beside Settings, to open Google / Cloud Save. If disconnected, connect with the account you want to delete.
  3. Choose Delete Cloud Account, then Confirm Delete.
  4. Verify with the same Google account. Wait for the message: “Cloud save and Jungle Hunt account deleted. Your device progress is kept.”

If the game says deletion did not finish, the request is not confirmed as complete. Cloud backup stays paused. Reconnect and retry, or use the email request above.

What successful cloud-account deletion removes

  • Your Jungle Hunt account in Firebase Authentication, including its associated sign-in profile.
  • Your active Firestore cloud save: chapter progress, stars, best results, coins, equipment upgrades, arrow inventory, selected arrow, tutorial status and saved preferences.
  • The account connection used by the game for cloud backup.

What remains and why

  • Your Google account remains. The game deletes only the Jungle Hunt account.
  • Progress on this device remains so you can continue playing locally. Other devices can also keep their own local copies.
  • Previously sent analytics, crash reports, advertising records and support email are not erased by the in-game button. These services use separate records and retention processes. Include them in your email request when relevant. Provider cleanup may continue after active account deletion; see the privacy policy's retention details.

Disconnect only signs you out and stops cloud backup. It does not delete the cloud account. Signing in again after deletion can create a new account and upload progress that remains on your device.

Clear progress from your device

In Android, open Settings > Apps > Jungle Hunt > Storage (or Storage & cache), then choose Clear storage / Clear data. This resets local chapter progress, coins, stars, arrows, upgrades and preferences. Menu names vary by device.

To remove both cloud and local progress, complete cloud-account deletion first, then clear storage on each device that has a copy. Check Android backup/restore settings if old progress returns. Clearing storage or uninstalling alone does not delete the cloud account or previously transmitted records.

Read the Jungle Hunt Privacy Policy. For help, contact sarkermit.apps@gmail.com.
