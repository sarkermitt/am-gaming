Jungle Hunt: device and cloud data

Jungle Hunt offers optional Google sign-in for cloud backup. You can delete your Jungle Hunt cloud save and account from inside the game:

  1. Return to Camp and tap the user icon in the top-right corner, beside Settings.
  2. On the Google / Cloud Save screen, choose Delete Cloud Account, then Confirm Delete.
  3. Verify with the same Google account used for your Jungle Hunt cloud save.

This removes your cloud progress, coins, inventory, upgrades, preferences and Firebase Authentication account. Your Google account and progress on this device are kept. If deletion cannot finish, reconnect and retry; cloud backup stays paused. Choosing Disconnect only signs you out and stops syncing; it does not delete cloud data.

To remove local progress, open Android Settings, find Jungle Hunt under Apps, open Storage (or Storage & cache) and choose Clear storage / Clear data. This resets your chapter progress, stars, coins, upgrades, arrow inventory and settings.

Device backup and restore settings may affect whether saved data returns later. Clearing local storage does not delete your cloud save, support emails or previously transmitted diagnostic records. Deleting the cloud account does not immediately erase previously transmitted analytics or crash records. For help with those requests, email sarkermit.apps@gmail.com with the game name and the information you want removed. For an account request, include the Google account email used for game sign-in so we can investigate and verify ownership. Never send your password or sign-in tokens.

If you cannot access the game, email sarkermit.apps@gmail.com to request Jungle Hunt account or data deletion. Include the account email and the data you want removed so we can verify ownership and handle the request. Never share a password or authentication code.

See the Jungle Hunt Privacy Policy for local and cloud saves, advertising, support contact, Firebase and Unity diagnostics information.

Math Puzzle IQ: account data

For Math Puzzle IQ data linked to your account, deletion requests are handled by email.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the address below from the email account you use to sign in to the game, and include:

  • The game name (e.g. Math Puzzle IQ)
  • The email / account you signed in with
  • The subject line "Data deletion request"
Send your request to
sarkermit.apps@gmail.com
✉️ Email your deletion request

What gets deleted from Math Puzzle IQ

We will permanently delete the game data associated with your account from our cloud storage, including your saved progress (levels, stars, coins, IQ score) and, if present, your profile name and photo. We'll confirm by email once it's done, normally within 30 days.

You can also remove data yourself

  • Sign out in the app to remove your profile from the device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

See the Math Puzzle IQ Privacy Policy for the full details of what we collect and why.
