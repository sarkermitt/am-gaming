Data Deletion Request

AM GAMING · Last updated: September 14, 2026

Choose the instructions for your game. Jungle Hunt keeps game progress on your device; Math Puzzle IQ can also store progress with a signed-in account.

Jungle Hunt: local game data

Jungle Hunt does not require an account and has no account-based cloud-save system. To remove local progress, open Android Settings, find Jungle Hunt under Apps, open Storage (or Storage & cache) and choose Clear storage / Clear data. This resets your chapter progress, stars, coins, upgrades, arrow inventory and settings.

Device backup and restore settings may affect whether saved data returns later. Clearing local storage does not delete support emails or any previously transmitted diagnostic records. For help with those requests, email sarkermit.play@gmail.com with the game name and the information you want removed; no game sign-in email is required.

See the Jungle Hunt Privacy Policy for local saves, support contact and Unity diagnostics information.

Math Puzzle IQ: account data

For Math Puzzle IQ data linked to your account, deletion requests are handled by email.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the address below from the email account you use to sign in to the game, and include:

  • The game name (e.g. Math Puzzle IQ)
  • The email / account you signed in with
  • The subject line "Data deletion request"
Send your request to
sarkermit.play@gmail.com
✉️ Email your deletion request

What gets deleted from Math Puzzle IQ

We will permanently delete the game data associated with your account from our cloud storage, including your saved progress (levels, stars, coins, IQ score) and, if present, your profile name and photo. We'll confirm by email once it's done, normally within 30 days.

You can also remove data yourself

  • Sign out in the app to remove your profile from the device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

See the Math Puzzle IQ Privacy Policy for the full details of what we collect and why.
