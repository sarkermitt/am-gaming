One bow. A jungle of challenges.

Every shot is your choice

Jungle Hunt is a single-player archery adventure through 20 different moonlit jungle environments. Face waves of flying creatures and ground enemies, find their weak spots and time your shots as colored bombs cross the arena. Your glowing arrows light up the battle.

Touch-controlled archery

Pull back to set your aim and draw strength, then release to fire. The aiming guide helps you read the arrow’s path. Eye hits deal extra damage.

A living night jungle

Explore moonlit forests, waterfalls and ancient ruins, with moving leaves, drifting clouds, jungle sounds and colored light from arrows and bombs.

Know your enemies

Use the Field Guide to learn about 54 creature species, their combat stats and tips for finding a weak spot. Face enemies in the air and on the ground.

Build your strength

Earn coins and up to three stars per chapter. Upgrade bow damage, health and reload speed in the Workshop. Shoot falling red life diamonds to recover health.

Choose your next shot

Four arrows. Different ways to fight.

Switch arrows using the circular buttons during battle. The counter shows how many special arrows remain.

Green · Unlimited

Classic

Your everyday arrow, surrounded by a green flame in flight. Always available with an unlimited supply and no coin cost.

Ice blue · Bomb defense

Crimson Wing

A wide icy effect follows this arrow. Use it to intercept and destroy enemy bombs before they reach the hero.

Red · Fast strike

Ember Fang

Flies 20% faster than Classic. A hit defeats the enemy in one shot and carries the enemy out of the arena with the arrow.

Fire orange · Piercing

Ruby Crescent

Pierces through an enemy in a single fatal hit. The enemy falls with a fiery effect while the arrow continues through the arena.

Buy special arrows in packs of 2, 5, 10 or 15 using coins earned through play. Each special shot uses one arrow. Tapping an empty arrow slot opens its purchase popup. These purchases use game coins, not real money.

Pull back. Aim. Release.

Hold your phone in landscape orientation and keep the next target in sight.

  1. Touch the play area

    The arrow loads when you begin your input. Start away from the pause and arrow-selection buttons.

  2. Pull back to draw

    The arrow flies opposite your swipe. Change the direction to aim and pull farther for more power.

  3. Release your shot

    Let go to fire. Wait for the reload, then line up your next shot. Try an eye hit for extra damage.

  4. Adapt as you fight

    Switch arrow types, intercept bombs with Crimson Wing and shoot red life diamonds to regain health.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-in is required. Your coins, stars, equipment, arrow inventory and settings are stored locally on your device.

Can I run out of arrows?

Classic arrows are unlimited. Special arrows have an ammunition count and use one per shot. Buy more with earned game coins, or switch back to Classic.

How do I unlock more chapters?

Complete the previous chapter to unlock the next. Revisit earlier chapters to improve your results and earn more stars.

How do I recover health?

Shoot the falling red life diamonds with an arrow to restore health. You can also improve your maximum health through the Workshop.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

The game has no account-based cloud-save system. Progress is stored on the current device; clearing app storage removes it. Android device backup and restore settings may affect what is retained.
