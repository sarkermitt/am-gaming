One bow. A jungle of challenges.

Every shot is your choice

Jungle Hunt is a single-player archery adventure with 200 chapters across 20 different moonlit jungle environments: 10 chapters in each setting, with a gradual increase in difficulty. Face waves of flying creatures and ground enemies, find their weak spots and time your shots as colored bombs cross the arena. Your glowing arrows light up the battle.

Touch-controlled archery

Pull back to set your aim and draw strength, then release to fire. The optional aiming guide helps you read the arrow’s path. Eye hits deal extra damage.

A living night jungle

Explore moonlit forests, waterfalls and ancient ruins, with moving leaves, drifting clouds, jungle sounds and colored light from arrows and bombs.

Know your enemies

Use the Field Guide to learn about 54 creature species, their combat stats and tips for finding a weak spot. Face enemies in the air and on the ground.

Build your strength

Earn coins and up to three stars per chapter. Upgrade bow damage, health and reload speed in the Workshop. Shoot falling red life diamonds to recover health.

20 night jungles · 10 chapters each

A journey through 200 chapters

Begin at Moonfern Gate and work toward Jungle Heart. Win a chapter to unlock the next, or return to an earlier chapter to improve your stars, shot count and completion time.

A gradual challenge

The difficulty indicator starts at 20% and rises to 80% across the campaign. Early chapters introduce smaller encounters; later chapters bring up to four waves. Every chapter is designed to be playable with the unlimited Classic arrow.

Earn up to 600 stars

Win without taking damage for three stars. After taking damage, finish with at least half your health for two stars; any other win earns one. Your best star result is kept.

Coins for your next hunt

A completed chapter awards 12 coins per defeated creature plus 30 coins per star. Replaying completed chapters also earns coins for ammunition and Workshop upgrades.

Choose your next shot

Four arrows. Different ways to fight.

Switch arrows using the circular buttons during battle. The counter shows how many special arrows remain.

Green · Unlimited

Classic

Your everyday arrow, surrounded by a green flame in flight. Always available with an unlimited supply and no coin cost.

Ice blue · Bomb defense

Crimson Wing

A wide icy effect follows this arrow. Use it to intercept and destroy enemy bombs before they reach the hero.

Red · Fast strike

Ember Fang

Flies 20% faster than Classic. A hit defeats the enemy in one shot and carries the enemy out of the arena with the arrow.

Fire orange · Piercing

Ruby Crescent

Pierces through an enemy in a single fatal hit. The enemy falls with a fiery effect while the arrow continues through the arena.

Buy special arrows in packs of 2, 5, 10 or 15 using coins earned through play. Each special shot uses one arrow. Tapping an empty arrow slot opens its purchase popup. These purchases use game coins, not real money.

Forge your equipment

Three upgrades for your ranger

Each item has three upgrade ranks, costing 90, 180 and 270 game coins. Upgrades stay with your saved progress.

Hunter’s Bow

Increase base bow damage by 6 per rank, from 34 to 52 at maximum rank. Draw strength and the hit location also affect a shot’s damage.

Ranger’s Cloak

Add 20 maximum health per rank, from 100 to 160. Falling red life diamonds restore up to 20 health when hit by your arrow, without exceeding your maximum.

Swift Quiver

Reduce the time between shots by 0.06 seconds per rank. Reload time drops from 0.50 seconds to 0.32 seconds at maximum rank.

Inside Jungle Hunt

See the adventure

In-game battles and the current 200-chapter Journey screen. Select an image to view it at full size. Battle screenshots are from development builds; chapter names and interface details may vary.

Pull back. Aim. Release.

Hold your phone in landscape orientation and keep the next target in sight.

  1. Touch the play area

    The arrow loads when you begin your input. Start away from the pause and arrow-selection buttons.

  2. Pull back to draw

    The arrow flies opposite your swipe. Change the direction to aim and pull farther for more power.

  3. Release your shot

    Let go to fire. Wait for the reload, then line up your next shot. Aim at the eye for extra damage, or hit a charging creature to interrupt its attack.

  4. Adapt as you fight

    Switch arrow types, intercept bombs with Crimson Wing and shoot red life diamonds to regain health.

Your journey, saved

Play offline. Connect when you choose.

Gameplay saves on your device without an account. Optional Google sign-in connects your cloud backup when you have internet.

  1. Open your account

    From Camp, tap the user icon in the top-right corner, beside Settings. Choose Sign in with Google on the Google / Cloud Save screen.

  2. Back up your progress

    Your chapter stars, best results, coins, upgrades, remaining arrows, equipped arrow and preferences are included. Use Sync Now to request an immediate backup.

  3. Continue on another device

    Use the same Google account. If the device and cloud have different saves, return to Camp and choose Keep Device Save or Keep Cloud Save. The selected save replaces the other; coins and arrows are not added together.

  4. Stay in control

    Disconnect stops cloud backup and keeps local progress. Delete Cloud Account removes your Jungle Hunt cloud account and save after confirmation. The back button returns you to Camp.

Cloud backup needs an internet connection. Signing out or clearing app storage does not delete your cloud save. See how to manage local and cloud data.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

You can play without signing in. Your coins, stars, equipment, arrow inventory and settings save on your device. Optional Google sign-in connects your cloud backup.

Can I run out of arrows?

Classic arrows are unlimited. Special arrows have an ammunition count and use one per shot. Buy more with earned game coins, or switch back to Classic.

How do I unlock more chapters?

Complete the previous chapter to unlock the next. Revisit earlier chapters to improve your results and earn more stars.

How do I recover health?

Shoot a falling red life diamond to recover up to 20 health. Diamonds are not destroyed by touching an enemy. Ranger’s Cloak upgrades increase your maximum health.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Tap the top-right user icon beside Settings in Camp and connect Google sign-in. Use the same Google account on your new Android device. Cloud backup needs internet. If the saves differ, choose which one to keep while in Camp.

When do ads appear?

A full-screen ad may appear after a won or lost chapter attempt, when one is available. Closing it returns you to the result screen. If an ad cannot load, you can continue. Ads do not award extra coins, arrows or health.

Do special arrows cost real money?

No. Special arrows and Workshop upgrades use coins earned through gameplay. Classic arrows are free and unlimited. Jungle Hunt does not have real-money in-app purchases.

Can I change the controls and effects?

Settings lets you toggle music, sound effects, the aiming guide and motion effects. You can also replay the tutorial. Where available, Ad Privacy lets you revisit advertising privacy choices.
